Security

A scanner that's careless about its own security would be embarrassing. Here's how we keep WebGuard (and you) safe.

Practice what we scan
TL;DR: We test gently, guard the scanner against misuse, protect your account, and welcome responsible bug reports.
🎯

What we look for

SQL Injection, reflected and stored XSS, and CSRF. We're focused, not all-knowing, and more checks may come later.

🪶

Gentle by design

The scanner sends test payloads and reports what it sees. It does not perform destructive exploitation, so it knocks on the door rather than kicking it in.

🧱

Guardrails on the scanner

Requests to private and internal addresses are blocked (anti-SSRF), and scan requests are rate limited, so WebGuard can't easily be turned into a weapon.

🔐

Your account

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Sessions use signed tokens, and you can sign in with Google instead of a password if you prefer.

🐛

Found a bug in WebGuard?

Awesome, and thank you! Please report it privately to the maintainers first (see contact) and give us a fair chance to fix it before sharing it publicly. No public shaming, just teamwork.

Security is a journey, and we're still learning. Thanks for helping us get better.
