Privacy Policy

We're a security tool, so snooping on you would be pretty awkward. Here's exactly what we keep, what we don't, and why.

Plain English edition
TL;DR: We keep what we need to run your scans and show your results. We don't sell your data, we don't run ads, and we never store the passwords you give us for scanning.

Your account

Sign up with email or Google and we store your email, your name (if you gave one) and a securely hashed password. "Hashed" means even we can't read it. If you use Google, we only receive the basics Google shares for sign-in.

Scan data

We save the target URL, scan status and findings so you can read your results later. It's your report card, so we keep it for you.

Credentials for authenticated scans

The login details you enter to scan behind a login page are used during that scan only. They're not kept as long-term records.

Cookies & sessions

No tracking cookies. Your login token lives in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in, and it's cleared when you log out. The scanner may use temporary session cookies while it logs in to your target.

Where it lives

Your data sits in the project's configured database. We don't share it with advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

Want it gone?

Ask and we'll delete your account and scan history. Find how on the contact page.

WebGuard is a student project, so this policy is written in good faith, not by a law firm.
